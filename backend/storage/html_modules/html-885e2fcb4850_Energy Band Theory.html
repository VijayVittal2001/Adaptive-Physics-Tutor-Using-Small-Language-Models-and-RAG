🎓 Board Exam Answer

According to energy band theory, electrons in solids occupy allowed energy bands. The band containing valence electrons is called the valence band, and the band above it is called the conduction band. The energy difference between them is called the energy gap or band gap.

In conductors, the valence band and conduction band overlap or the conduction band is partially filled, so electrons move freely. In semiconductors, the band gap is small, so some electrons can jump to the conduction band at room temperature. In insulators, the band gap is large, so electrons cannot easily move to the conduction band.
